## **Contingency Plans**

### **If Main Vendor No-Shows (Catering/AV)**
- Catering: Contact backup caterer (Savory Selections, 336-555-1234) by 2 PM
- AV: Contact backup tech (Local AV Rental, 336-555-5678) by 2 PM
- Notify Carrie immediately, adjust timeline as needed

### **If Severe Weather**
- Venue has indoor backup area (gym can accommodate 100 if outdoor areas unusable)
- All activities move indoors, condensed timeline
- Notify guests via email/phone by 3 PM day-of

### **If Keynote Speaker Cancels**
- Executive Director delivers extended remarks (instead of 5 min, 12 min)
- Board member shares impact story (5 min prepared remarks ready)
- No material change to event flow

### **If Tech Fails During Remarks**
- Carrie/speakers project voice to back of room (no microphone)
- AV tech troubleshoots in parallel
- If video fails, distribute printed handouts with same impact data

### **If Fewer Guests Attend Than Expected**
- Notify Trenton Events by 4:45 PM (can reduce plate count for refund)
- Adjust table arrangements to appear well-populated
- No change to program or timeline
